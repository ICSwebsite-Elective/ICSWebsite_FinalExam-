OUR MISSION

The Black Panthers – Information Technology aims to empower first-year IT students by building a strong foundation in technology, problem-solving, and collaboration. We are committed to fostering discipline, innovation, and excellence while nurturing teamwork, leadership, and ethical responsibility. As future IT professionals, we strive to develop skills that adapt to evolving technologies and contribute meaningful solutions to our institution and society.
