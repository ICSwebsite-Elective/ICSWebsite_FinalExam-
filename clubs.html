Our Mission

To cultivate a community of passionate tech enthusiasts who innovate, collaborate, and lead in the fields of software development, robotics, and information technology, while fostering skills, creativity, and a spirit of lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To empower students to become innovative technology leaders, equipped with the skills, creativity, and collaboration mindset to shape the future of the digital world and make a meaningful impact in the IT community.

Activities & Achievements

  • Organizing coding bootcamps and workshops
  • Participation in inter-school hackathons and competitions
  • Community outreach and tech mentorship programs
  • Showcasing innovative student projects

Registration

Interested in joining InfoTech? Please follow the instructions below to register as a member:

  • Fill out the registration form completely.
  • Submit a valid student ID or proof of enrollment.
  • Agree to the InfoTech code of conduct.
  • Wait for confirmation from the officers.

After submitting your registration, the InfoTech officers will review your application and send a confirmation email.
